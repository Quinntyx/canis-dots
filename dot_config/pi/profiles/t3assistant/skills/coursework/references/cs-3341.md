# Quantitative study with restricted assignment assistance

1. Read the current course assistance policy. When generated assigned answers or solution manuals
   are prohibited, treat the actual homework as human work and provide its organizational handoff.
2. For an explicit permitted study request, explain the underlying definitions and methods using
   unrelated original examples. Verify examples do not merely substitute numbers into the assigned
   problem or reveal its solution strategy in violation of the policy.
3. Give an independent practice question and an observable self-check. Keep that study note separate
   from the assigned answer sheet; record remaining conceptual gaps and a review date.
4. Leave assigned answers and reference answer sheets to the student under the course's rules.
   For unclear restrictions, obtain the actual policy before expanding the assistance scope.

Automated discovery should produce an idle reminder for policy-restricted assigned work. A
submission or grade never proves that the underlying probability/statistics concepts are learned.
