# Disclosed debugging and testing of student-written work

1. Establish the current assignment policy and the user's specific debugging/testing request.
   Require the student's existing attempt, observed failure, and expected behavior before diagnosis.
2. When the policy permits only debugging/testing, explain the defect and propose a focused test
   or diagnostic experiment. Have the student make the correction and explain why it works.
3. Keep theory answers, replacement implementations, and generated assignment/report text out of
   this restricted route. General concept study uses unrelated examples under the verified policy.
4. Record the help and disclosure required by the course, the test outcome actually observed, and
   a remaining independent practice step. Do not claim grading or submission success.

Automatic scheduling uses an idle human-work reminder until the user supplies work and explicitly
asks for permitted assistance. This procedure does not turn an assignment title into that request.
