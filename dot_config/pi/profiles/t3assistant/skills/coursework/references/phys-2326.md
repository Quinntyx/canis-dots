# Physics homework and assessment preparation

1. Verify the actual course offering, topic, and assessment type. Separate homework from timed
   quizzes, exams, in-class attendance and fixed participation windows.
2. Hand off live timed or attendance activities with a verified access window and activity link.
   Prepare requested study materials separately without starting or submitting the activity.
3. Use actual slides/textbook to identify governing laws, assumptions, diagrams, units and limiting
   cases. Prepare worked examples, independent checks, and separately requested reference answers
   with reasoning and source citations.
4. Reuse the [mathematical preparation](math-3351.md) artifact pattern, keeping the final student
   answer sheet separate. For experimental work use the [lab route](phys-2126.md).

Do not infer an assignment's availability or current enrollment from the feed or a course number.
