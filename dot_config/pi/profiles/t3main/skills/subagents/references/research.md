# Parallel research

1. Turn the question into independent evidence objectives with a shared decision criterion.
   Separate complementary investigations from deliberate independent verification.
2. Assign each worker its question, known context, permitted sources/tools and expected evidence
   artifact. Request exact source URLs, revision or publication identity, relevant quotations or
   code locations, the conclusion they support, and remaining uncertainty.
3. Keep source material in durable artifacts and return compact findings with pointers. Search
   primary sources first, inspect the retrieved content and distinguish observation from inference.
4. Consume results as they arrive. Consolidate duplicate claims by source and underlying evidence;
   retain disagreement as an explicit unresolved question rather than averaging confidence.
5. Release follow-up research only for a decision-relevant gap. Scale the roster to independent
   search space and source cost; let one worker own a tightly coupled question.
6. Validate decisive citations in their actual source. Synthesize the answer around the requested
   decision, supported alternatives and uncertainty, with enough references for audit.
7. Finish when the decision criterion is supported or the remaining gap is a reported blocker.
   Keep hosted posting, code edits and implementation as separately authorized downstream work.

# Coordination

- The coordinator owns scope, deduplication and synthesis. Workers retain freedom to explore their
  evidence objective and can propose follow-ups as results rather than recursively scheduling them.
- Delegate tasks are one-shot: resolve a source discrepancy with a follow-up child whose brief
  carries the prior child's summary, exact source references and artifact paths so that evidence
  survives summarization.
- Use `../model-selection/SKILL.md` for evidence-worker and frontier-synthesis selection when
  role routing is needed. Simple delegation can inherit a capable selected-profile model.
  Verify consequential claims against the underlying evidence.
