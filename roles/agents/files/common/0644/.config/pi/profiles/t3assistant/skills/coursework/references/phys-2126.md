# Physics lab preparation

1. Verify the actual lab instructions, attendance/window requirements, and safety guidance.
   Identify the measurements and observations needed for the requested preparation or analysis.
2. Prepare a source-linked equipment/procedure checklist and an empty data table or report template
   as requested. Explain relevant physics and uncertainty analysis with clearly synthetic examples.
3. Record measured data only from the student's actual observations or explicitly supplied files.
   Preserve units, provenance and raw values; never fabricate measurements or attendance.
4. Help analyze supplied data and prepare requested explanations or report drafts. The student
   performs the lab and chooses what to submit. Hand off time-fixed or locked activities with
   their verified access window rather than attempting them autonomously.
5. Record unresolved experimental/conceptual questions as learning debt separately from submission.
